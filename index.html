How to Play

1. Setup: Sit around a table (3-6 players).
2. Action: Draw 1 card per turn.
3. Response: You may Answer, Pass, or Respond Abstractly.
4. Goal: No winning, no scoring. The goal is simply to finish the deck or stop when it feels right.

Remember: Silence is always a valid answer.
